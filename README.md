I did not have enough time to complete fully. I had to scrap some features and only have the tutorial level in the final. Click your character to respawn at beginning a maximum of 3 times. A ghost is placed at the place where you died last. That can be used to jump off of. Green ghosts are basic platforms while blue ghosts bounce you up. Theme: Bounce Back. You bounce back to life and can use your past experience/life to help you progress in the level.


play this game at: https://firexfiregod.itch.io/bounceback
